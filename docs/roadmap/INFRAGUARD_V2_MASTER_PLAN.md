# InfraGuard v2.0 Customer-Centric Master Strategy & Product Plan

> **Product Core Vision:** InfraGuard helps SMB IT teams quickly find network problems, understand why they happened, and know what to do next — using verified information in simple language.

---

## 🎯 The Core Customer Journey

InfraGuard does not present raw network protocols to non-experts. The entire product workflow strictly follows the **5-Step SMB IT Support Journey**:

$$\text{Find Devices} \longrightarrow \text{Check Health} \longrightarrow \text{Understand Problem} \longrightarrow \text{Suggest Solution} \longrightarrow \text{Generate Report} \longrightarrow \text{Keep Watching}$$

---

## 🚀 15-Phase Customer-Centric Product Roadmap

| Phase | Phase Name | Customer Goal & User Value | Core Features & Output |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Find Devices** | *"What devices are on my network?"* | Simple "Find Devices" button. Auto-runs ICMP/ARP/mDNS in background. |
| **02** | **Device Details** | *"Who owns this IP?"* | Simple 1-page profile (Device Name, IP, MAC, Brand, Category, Verified Star Rating). |
| **03** | **Troubleshooting Assistant**| *"Why is this device failing?"* (USP) | Instant "Possible Reasons" (Power/Cable) + "What You Can Try Next" action cards. |
| **04** | **Live Monitoring** | *"Is my network stable right now?"* | 60-second background polling (Latency, Packet Loss, Bandwidth, Uptime). |
| **05** | **Alert Engine** | *"Notify me when something breaks."* | Real-time incident stream (Printer Offline, Gateway Unreachable, Storage Full). |
| **06** | **Executive Reports** | *"Provide proof of IT health to manager."*| Simple Executive PDF Report for Managers vs Technical Audit PDF for Engineers. |
| **07** | **Network Map** | *"Visual layout of my office setup."* | Simple hierarchical visualizer (Internet → Firewall → Switch → Connected Assets). |
| **08** | **Device History** | *"When did this intermittent drop start?"* | Interactive timeline ("Yesterday 10:40 AM Latency Drop → 11:05 AM Recovered"). |
| **09** | **Maintenance & Asset Reminders**| *"When does hardware warranty expire?"* | Maintenance alerts (UPS Battery health, Warranty 30 days left, NAS Disk SMART). |
| **10** | **Simple Network Health Score**| *"How healthy is my office today?"* | 4-Tier Health Score: Excellent (95/100), Good, Needs Attention, Critical. |
| **11** | **ISP & Internet Health Check** | *"Is my internet slow or is it internal?"*| ISP latency probe, DNS resolution test, top bandwidth usage breakdown. |
| **12** | **Daily Office Briefing** | *"Morning IT Summary"* | "Good Morning! 24 Devices Scanned. 23 Healthy, 1 Printer Offline. No security issues." |
| **13** | **Multi-Site & InfraGuard Beacon**| *"Manage main office and branch stores."*| Windows Service collector sending encrypted telemetry to central dashboard. |
| **14** | **Mobile Management App** | *"Troubleshoot from smartphone."* | Instant push alerts and 1-tap troubleshooting guidance on iOS / Android. |
| **15** | **AI Troubleshooting Assistant** | *"Predictive root-cause correlation."* | *(Future v4.0)* "Printer offline 3 times this week at 10:40 AM. Check power cable." |

## 💼 Commercial Pilot Strategy & Go-To-Market

Before public commercial launch, InfraGuard will execute a **3-Client Local SMB Pilot Program**:

```text
Phase A: 3 Local SMB Free Trials (Local Offices / Clinics / Warehouses)
   ↓
Phase B: Gather Real-World Feedback & Validate Custom Router/Printer Hardware
   ↓
Phase C: Fix Edge-Case Bugs & Build Case Studies / Video Walkthroughs
   ↓
Phase D: Paid Commercial Launch (Starter $29/mo, Pro $79/mo, MSP Tier)
```
